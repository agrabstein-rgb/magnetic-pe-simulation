Selected interaction: Attraction

What the graph means

Attraction: pulling magnets apart increases PE. Repulsion: pushing magnets together increases PE. Watch whether the single bar grows or shrinks.

Model limitation

This simplified classroom model uses U = 20 − 40/r for attraction and U = 20 + 40/r for repulsion. Distance r is in cm; PE uses arbitrary units (a.u.). Adding 20 to both curves shifts the zero reference so no negative values appear; it does not change which direction PE increases or decreases. Real bar magnets do not generally follow a 1/r energy law.

Student exploration: At 4 cm, record each interaction's PE. Repeat at 12 cm. Which interaction stores more PE when the magnets are brought closer? What evidence on the graph supports your answer?
